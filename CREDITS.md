## 3D experience architecture

The interactive 3D structure of this portfolio (single R3F canvas, in-scene
navigation, the animated laptop / projects / contact scenes and camera
choreography) is adapted from **Eli Parker's** open-source interactive portfolio:

- Source: https://github.com/Eli-Parker/eli-parker.github.io
- Author: Eli Parker (https://eliparker.dev)
- License: MIT

It has been ported from Vite to Next.js, restyled for the Vice City neon theme,
stripped of its debug tooling, and repopulated with Maharshi Barot's own content.
